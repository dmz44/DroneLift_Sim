Switching runtime
=================

Modes
-----

The system runs one of three controllers, ordered by mission capability
:math:`\mathrm{HP} \succ \mathrm{SC} \succ \mathrm{FS}`.

**HP — high-performance controller.** Keeps tracking the payload reference
:math:`p_{\mathrm{ref}}(t)`. Until it is reconfigured it is *stale*: it still assumes
the pre-failure team, so each survivor carries only its old share :math:`m_L/N` of the load.
Once reconfigured, it moves the survivors at once to a regular polygon
(Alg. *Reform*) and continues at full speed.

**SC — safety controller.** Damps the swing, reforms the polygon along synchronized
paths over :math:`T_b` (separation is preserved, Lemma *Separation*), and rejoins a
time-warped reference :math:`p_{\mathrm{ref}}(\sigma(t))`. In the paper its parameters
come from the learned model ML-2. Here they are sliders, with defaults
:math:`T_b = 2.5` s, :math:`\theta_{\mathrm{SC}} = 35^\circ`, :math:`k_s = 0.8` and the
reference frozen (the hand-set vector of the paper's swing study).

**FS — failsafe controller.** A simple, non-learned controlled descent. It stops
horizontally, reforms, and lowers the payload with bounded acceleration until touchdown,
then holds the drones above it on slack cables.

Failure and switching timeline
------------------------------

At :math:`t_f` the drones in :math:`F` crash (zero thrust). Their cables are released after
:math:`\Delta_{\mathrm{rel}}`. Until then each one hangs on the payload as dead weight. The runtime
detects the failure after :math:`\Delta_{\mathrm{det}}`, decides after
:math:`\Delta_{\mathrm{dec}}`, and hands over after :math:`\Delta_{\mathrm{ho}}`. The
new controller's command is then blended in over :math:`T_{\mathrm{bl}} = 200` ms. The
switching delay is

.. math::

   \Delta = \Delta_{\mathrm{det}} + \Delta_{\mathrm{dec}} + \Delta_{\mathrm{ho}}
   = (50 + 10 + 20)\,\mathrm{ms} \times s_\Lambda\, j .

Switching budget and decision rule
----------------------------------

The **switching budget** :math:`\tau_m` of mode :math:`m` is the longest switching delay
after which the team, running the stale HP until the switch and mode :math:`m` afterwards,
still satisfies the success predicate. The paper learns :math:`\hat\tau_m` with ML-1
from labels generated by sweeping the handover time in simulation. The browser computes
those labels directly. For each mode it re-runs the scenario from the pre-failure state at
delays :math:`\{0.02, 0.04, \dots, 1.92\}` s and records the largest delay before the
first failure. The decision rule is

.. math::

   m^\star = \max\nolimits_{\succ}\bigl\{ m \in \{\mathrm{HP}, \mathrm{SC}\} :
   \tau_m \ge \Delta + \lambda \bigr\}, \quad \text{otherwise FS},

with decision margin :math:`\lambda \ge 0`.

.. note::

   Because the browser uses the label generator itself in place of ML-1's prediction,
   "Simplex" on this site has a perfect selector. It is an upper bound on selector quality.
   Safety still rests on the trusted core: if the selector is wrong, the watchdogs below
   downgrade the mode, and FS is non-learned.

Watchdogs
---------

* **W1 (guards HP).** Requests SC when the tracking error exceeds
  :math:`\delta_{\mathrm{HP}} - \mu_{\mathrm{pos}}`, or when separation or height comes
  within a small margin of (S1)/(S2). A W1 trigger before the decision caps the decision at SC.
* **W2 (guards SC).** Requests FS when the error exceeds
  :math:`\delta_{\mathrm{SC}} - \mu_{\mathrm{pos}}`, when the delay exceeds
  :math:`D_{\max} - \mu_{\mathrm{del}}`, or on the same separation and height margins.

Modes only move downward. Every watchdog switch pays the handover latency and the blend.
The baselines (high-level only, always SC, always FS) run without watchdogs.

Success predicate
-----------------

A run succeeds if, from :math:`t_f` to :math:`t_f + T_{\mathrm h}`, every surviving drone
satisfies (S1) separation, (S2) height and (S3) tilt, no surviving drone or payload touches
the ground outside FS, and the final mode keeps its promise:

* **HP:** payload within :math:`\delta_{\mathrm{HP}}` of :math:`p_{\mathrm{ref}}(t)` throughout.
* **SC:** payload within :math:`\delta_{\mathrm{SC}}` of :math:`p_{\mathrm{ref}}(\sigma(t))`, with delay
  :math:`t - \sigma(t) \le D_{\max}`.
* **FS:** the SC tube holds until FS takes over, then the payload's downward acceleration stays
  :math:`\le \bar a` until touchdown (S6). This also bounds the touchdown speed by
  :math:`\sqrt{\dot z_0^2 + 2\bar a h_0}`.
